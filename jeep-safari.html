Regional Exploration Guide

Popular Visitor Sectors & Entry Gate Distances from Ramnagar

Learn about the different forest sectors around Ramnagar and Kotdwar, including their distance from town, terrain features, and seasonal calendar to help plan your private Gypsy transfer.

1. Bijrani Sector

Prized for its tall Sal woodlands, sunlit Chaurs (grasslands), and wide dry stream beds, Bijrani is one of the most popular sectors for nature lovers. Reached via Amdanda Gate (about 2 km from Ramnagar town), it welcomes visitors from mid-October to 30th June and rests during the monsoon rains. Guests frequently spot spotted deer, sambar, elephants, sloth bears, and big cats.

2. Jhirna Sector

Set along the southern edge of the landscape, Jhirna features dry deciduous scrub, bamboo thickets, and broad pebbled stream crossings that offer wide viewing angles. Reached via the Dhela / Khara route (~16 km from Ramnagar), Jhirna remains open throughout most of the year depending on weather conditions, and is well known for wild elephant herds, sloth bears, nilgai, and hornbills.

3. Dhela Sector

Developed as a dedicated eco-tourism sector in 2014, Dhela blends Sal and Rohini trees with open meadow pockets, quiet waterholes, and wooden viewing towers. Reached via Dhela Gate (~15 km from Ramnagar), this sector stays accessible across most months (weather permitting) and supports leopards, tigers, barking deer, and over 300 species of resident and winter birds.

4. Garjiya (Ringora) Sector

Nestled near the Kosi river valley, Garjiya offers rolling woodland trails, dense Sal shade, and scenic forest ridges. Visitors travel via Ringora / Garjiya Gate (~11 km from Ramnagar) between mid-November and 30th June, giving travellers opportunities to spot deer herds, wild boars, jackals, forest eagles, and big cats.

5. Phato Sector

Located in the lush Terai West woodland belt, Phato is appreciated for its towering Sal canopies, tranquil waterholes, scenic watchtowers, and calm trails away from busy highway belts. Reached via the Phato / Mohan route (~22 km from Ramnagar), it stays open for most of the year and shelters elephants, leopards, tigers, spotted deer, and woodland birds.

6. Durga Devi Sector

Positioned in the hilly northeastern Shivalik terrain, Durga Devi follows the scenic valleys of the Ramganga and Mandal rivers—a favourite landscape for birdwatchers and nature photographers. Reached via Durga Devi Gate (~28 km from Ramnagar) from mid-November to mid-June, it is home to wild elephants, leopards, tigers, river otters, and crested serpent eagles.

7. Sitabani Woodland Trail

Situated in the adjoining Ramnagar woodland belt, Sitabani combines peaceful Sal and bamboo groves, shallow streams, and ancient heritage shrines. Reached via Teda or Paulgarh Gate (~5–12 km from Ramnagar), Sitabani remains open year-round for scenic Gypsy drives, birding walks, and spotting deer, elephants, and kingfishers.

8. Sonanadi Sector

Stretching across the northwestern Pauri Garhwal foothills, the Sonanadi landscape features dense Sal and Sheesham forests where the Palain and Mandal streams meet the Ramganga waters. Note: Sonanadi (Vatanvasa / Halduparao Gate) is approached from the Kotdwar side rather than Ramnagar. Open from mid-November to mid-June, it serves as a major corridor for Asian elephants and foothill birdlife.

9. Pakhro Sector

Part of the western Sonanadi eco-tourism belt, Pakhro offers quiet Bhabar-Terai Sal woodland, perennial streams, and unhurried forest tracks. Like Sonanadi, the approach point for Pakhro is located on the Kotdwar / Lansdowne side, making it convenient for travellers arriving from western Uttarakhand. It supports elephants, deer species, wild boars, and rich sub-Himalayan flora.

*Helpful Travel Note on Dhikala: Day-visit private Gypsy drives do not operate inside the Dhikala meadow sector for visitors staying at outside resorts. Day travellers explore Dhikala via the 16-Seater Canter Excursion, while private Gypsy transfers for Dhikala are arranged for guests with confirmed overnight stays.

Daily Excursion Windows

4x4 Gypsy Drive Timings & Pickup Schedule

Open Gypsy excursions operate in two daily shifts—Morning and Afternoon. Our driver arrives at your resort in Ramnagar or Dhikuli 30–45 minutes prior to your shift.

🌅 Morning Shift (Sunrise Drive)

  • Winter (October to February): ~6:00 AM / 6:30 AM to 9:30 AM / 10:00 AM
  • Summer (March to June): ~5:30 AM / 6:00 AM to 9:00 AM / 9:30 AM

🌇 Afternoon Shift (Late-Day Drive)

  • Winter (October to February): ~1:30 PM / 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM / 5:30 PM
  • Summer (March to June): ~2:30 PM / 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM / 6:30 PM
