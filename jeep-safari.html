The Classic 4x4 Gypsy Experience

Why Travellers Prefer an Open Jeep Drive in Corbett

An open-top 4x4 Maruti Gypsy excursion offers a quiet, personalized way to experience the winding woodland tracks, tall Sal groves, seasonal pebble stream beds (sots), and open meadows of the Corbett landscape. Accompanied by an experienced local driver and a trained nature guide, your group can pause along forest clearings, listen to woodland birdcalls, and observe fresh animal tracks along sandy trails.

Each Gypsy comfortably seats up to 6 adult guests (plus up to 2 young children under 5 years), providing clear all-round views for nature lovers and wildlife photographers. Daily vehicle entries follow fixed sector capacities to keep the forest peaceful, and all morning and afternoon drives conclude before dusk.

Corbett Exploration Sectors

Where Do Open Jeeps Go? (Visitor Sectors & Entry Gates)

The Corbett landscape features several distinct exploration sectors, each with its own entry gate, forest character, and seasonal visiting calendar.

1. Bijrani Sector

Prized for its tall Sal woodlands, sunlit Chaurs (grasslands), and wide dry stream beds, Bijrani is one of the most sought-after sectors for wildlife observation. Reached via Amdanda Gate (about 2 km from Ramnagar town), it welcomes visitors from mid-October to 30th June and rests during the monsoon rains. Guests frequently spot spotted deer, sambar, elephants, sloth bears, and big cats.

2. Jhirna Sector

Set along the southern edge of the reserve, Jhirna features dry deciduous scrub, bamboo thickets, and broad pebbled stream crossings that offer wide viewing angles. Entered through the Dhela / Khara route (~16 km from Ramnagar), Jhirna remains open throughout most of the year depending on weather conditions, and is well known for wild elephant herds, sloth bears, nilgai, and hornbills.

3. Dhela Sector

Added as a dedicated eco-tourism sector in 2014, Dhela blends Sal and Rohini trees with open meadow pockets, quiet waterholes, and wooden viewing towers. Reached via Dhela Gate (~15 km from Ramnagar), this sector stays accessible across most months (weather permitting) and supports leopards, tigers, barking deer, and over 300 species of resident and winter birds.

4. Garjiya (Ringora) Sector

Nestled between Bijrani and Dhikala near the Kosi river valley, Garjiya offers rolling woodland trails, dense Sal shade, and scenic forest ridges. Open Jeeps enter through Ringora / Garjiya Gate (~11 km from Ramnagar) between mid-November and 30th June, giving travellers opportunities to spot deer herds, wild boars, jackals, forest eagles, and big cats.

5. Phato Sector

Located in the lush Terai West woodland division, Phato is appreciated for its towering Sal canopies, tranquil forest waterholes, scenic watchtowers, and calm trails away from busy highway belts. Entered via Phato / Mohan route (~22 km from Ramnagar), it stays open for most of the year and shelters elephants, leopards, tigers, spotted deer, and woodland birds.

6. Durga Devi Sector

Positioned in the hilly northeastern Shivalik terrain, Durga Devi follows the scenic valleys of the Ramganga and Mandal rivers—a favourite landscape for birdwatchers and nature photographers. Entered through Durga Devi Gate (~28 km from Ramnagar) from mid-November to mid-June, it is home to wild elephants, leopards, tigers, river otters, and crested serpent eagles.

7. Sitabani Woodland Trail

Situated in the adjoining Ramnagar woodland division outside the inner reserve boundary, Sitabani combines peaceful Sal and bamboo groves, shallow streams, and ancient heritage shrines. Entered via Teda or Paulgarh Gate (~5–12 km from Ramnagar), Sitabani remains open year-round for scenic drives, birding walks, and spotting deer, elephants, and kingfishers.

8. Sonanadi Sector

Stretching across the northwestern Pauri Garhwal foothills, the Sonanadi landscape features dense Sal and Sheesham forests where the Palain and Mandal streams meet the Ramganga waters. Note: Entry to Sonanadi (Vatanvasa / Halduparao Gate) is approached from the Kotdwar side rather than Ramnagar. Open from mid-November to mid-June, it serves as a major corridor for Asian elephants and foothill birdlife.

9. Pakhro Sector

Part of the western Sonanadi eco-tourism belt, Pakhro offers quiet Bhabar-Terai Sal woodland, perennial streams, and unhurried forest tracks. Like Sonanadi, the entry point for Pakhro is located on the Kotdwar / Lansdowne side, making it convenient for travellers arriving from western Uttarakhand. It supports elephants, deer species, wild boars, and rich sub-Himalayan flora.

*Helpful Note on Dhikala Sector: Private day Jeep drives do not operate inside the Dhikala meadow sector for visitors staying at outside resorts. Day guests explore Dhikala aboard the 16-Seater Canter Excursion, while private Jeep exploration inside Dhikala is available exclusively for guests staying overnight at the Dhikala Forest Lodge.

Daily Excursion Windows

Jim Corbett Jeep Drive Timings & Departure Tips

Open Jeep excursions run in two daily slots—Morning and Afternoon. Being ready at your resort or entry gate 30 minutes early with your photo ID ensures a smooth departure.

🌅 Morning Slot (Sunrise Drive)

  • Winter (October to February): ~6:00 AM / 6:30 AM to 9:30 AM / 10:00 AM
  • Summer (March to June): ~5:30 AM / 6:00 AM to 9:00 AM / 9:30 AM
  • Why Guests Prefer It: Fresh morning forest air, clear tracks on sandy trails, active woodland birds, and soft golden light.

🌇 Afternoon Slot (Late-Day Drive)

  • Winter (October to February): ~1:30 PM / 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM / 5:30 PM
  • Summer (March to June): ~2:30 PM / 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM / 6:30 PM
  • Why Guests Prefer It: Warm afternoon temperatures often encourage elephants, deer, and big cats to visit shaded streams and waterholes.

📋 Visitor Checklist & Gate Tips

  • Carry Valid Photo ID: Bring the same original photo ID card shared during trip planning for gate check-in.
  • Nature Etiquette: Because wildlife moves freely in natural forest habitats, sightings are never guaranteed—enjoy the forest with patience and care.
  • Convenient Ramnagar Pickup: Your 4x4 Gypsy arrives at your Ramnagar or Dhikuli resort 30–45 minutes before gate departure.
Simple 4-Step Planning

How to Plan Your Jim Corbett Jeep Excursion

Daily vehicle slots in each sector fill up quickly during holidays and weekends. Follow these 4 simple steps to organize your private Gypsy transfer and stay smoothly.

Step 1: Pick Your Date & Slot

Select your travel dates and decide between the morning sunrise drive or afternoon slot. Planning 30 to 45 days ahead is ideal for weekends.

Step 2: Choose Your Sector

Select a sector that matches your itinerary—Bijrani for Sal groves and meadows, Jhirna or Dhela for year-round trails, Garjiya near Kosi, or Phato.

Step 3: Share Guest Details

Keep guest names, ages, and photo ID details ready (up to 6 adults per Gypsy) and carry the same original ID card on the day of your excursion.

Step 4: Ramnagar Pickup & Drive

On your scheduled day, your private 4x4 open Gypsy picks you up from your nearby Ramnagar or Dhikuli resort and transfers you to your designated gate.
