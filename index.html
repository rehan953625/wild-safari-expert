Heart of the Shivalik Foothills

The Ramnagar & Corbett Landscape: Setting & Character

Where the Himalayan foothills meet the northern plains, creating one of India's richest habitats for woodland flora, birdlife, and wild mammals.

The Corbett foothills landscape sits in the scenic sub-Himalayan belt of Uttarakhand, close to the lively gateway town of Ramnagar. Spanning parts of Nainital and Pauri Garhwal districts, this region marks the natural meeting point of the Shivalik hills and the fertile Bhabar-Terai belt. Because of this varied terrain, visitors experience everything from shaded hill valleys to wide, sunlit riverbeds in a single trip.

Across the broader forest belt, dense Sal woodlands blend into open grass meadows, pebble-strewn streams, and tranquil river pools. These interconnected habitats support diverse wildlife including the Royal Bengal Tiger, Asian Elephant, Leopard, Spotted Deer (Chital), and Sambar. Bird enthusiasts also prize the region for its remarkable variety of woodland, wetland, and Himalayan foothill birds.

🌲 Diverse Woodland

Moist Sal forests, bamboo clusters, and mixed deciduous canopies that shift colors with every season.

🌊 Dual River Valleys

Nourished by the winding Ramganga and Kosi rivers, forming natural lifelines for aquatic and forest life.

🌾 Open Grass Meadows

Locally known as Chaurs, these expansive grazing grounds offer clear views across the valley floor.

🦅 Rich Avian Habitat

Over 600 recorded bird species ranging from crested eagles and hornbills to vibrant river kingfishers.

Terrain, Rivers & Travel Preparation

Understanding the Foothills Ecosystem Before You Travel

A closer look at how the Himalayan foothills, river corridors, and seasonal shifts shape your excursion and stay experience around Ramnagar.

⛰️ Foothills & Bhabar-Terai Belt

The region lies where the Shivalik hills meet the northern plains. Around Ramnagar, gentle farmland quickly transitions into wooded slopes, pebble-filled stream beds (sots), and shaded valleys.

The porous Bhabar soil absorbs hill streams during dry months, while the adjoining Terai belt retains rich moisture—creating side-by-side pockets of dry scrub, tall woodland, and lush marshland.

🌿 Sal Forests & Open Chaurs

Towering Sal trees form the signature canopy across much of the foothills, mingled with haldu, Kusum, bamboo, and flowering flame-of-the-forest trees in spring.

Between wooded ridges lie flat grassland clearings called chaurs. These open meadows provide rich grazing for spotted deer, sambar, and elephant herds, especially during cool mornings and late afternoons.

💧 Ramganga & Kosi River Lifelines

The Ramganga River winds through the heart of the valley before opening into the wide Kalagarh reservoir, supporting fish eagles, waterbirds, mugger crocodiles, and sleek gharials.

Along the eastern edge near Dhikuli and Ramnagar, the boulder-strewn Kosi River forms a scenic backdrop for riverside resorts and winter birdwatching walks.

🐾 Wildlife & Birding Diversity

While many guests hope to spot the Royal Bengal Tiger, the forest is equally memorable for herds of Asian elephants, elusive leopards, jackals, sloth bears, and barking deer.

Carrying a pair of binoculars rewards travellers with sightings of great hornbills, crested serpent eagles, plum-headed parakeets, and colourful river kingfishers along quiet tracks.

🧭 Choosing Between Jeep & Canter

An open 4x4 Jeep accommodates up to six travellers and navigates narrower woodland loops across sectors like Bijrani, Jhirna, Dhela, Garjiya, Durga Devi, and Phato.

A 16-seater open Canter follows the main Dhangarhi-to-Dhikala corridor, giving day visitors a comfortable way to experience the deep Dhikala meadows without an overnight lodge stay.

🎒 Smart Packing & Forest Etiquette

Dress in earth-toned layers (olive, beige, brown, or grey) so you blend naturally with the woodland surroundings, and pack a warm windproof jacket for misty winter mornings.

Keep voices low during drives, switch phones to silent mode, avoid littering, and let your driver maintain a respectful, unhurried distance from wildlife.
