2. Park Entry Schedules & Advance Reservation Guidelines

Important Note on Entry Bookings & Lodge Schedules:

Wild Safari (wildsafariexpert.online), operated by Rahino (GSTIN: 05FIKPR6535Q1ZB), operates strictly as an independent private travel, resort stay, and vehicle coordinator, and is not affiliated with the Uttarakhand Forest Department or the official Corbett Tiger Reserve portal.

Visitor entry slots and interior forest rest houses are regulated by park authorities; we do not sell standalone official permits or charge any markup on statutory entry tariffs. Our commercial package charges cover private 4x4 Gypsy rental, local driver, resort pickup/drop-off transfers, and dedicated itinerary assistance.

  • Fixed Visitor Details: Once an entry slot is confirmed for a specific date and time window, guest names, dates, and sectors cannot be modified, swapped, or transferred to another person or date.
  • Valid Photo ID at Entry Gate: Every guest should carry the exact original photo ID card shared during trip planning. Accurate guest details ensure smooth check-in at the designated entry point.
  • Sector Allocation: Visitor sectors (such as Bijrani, Jhirna, Dhela, Garjiya, Durga Devi, Phato, or Dhikala) depend on seasonal availability at the time of planning.

3. Local Nature Guide Arrangement (Direct Gate Payment)

Nature Guide Fee is Paid Directly at the Entry Gate:

Every excursion drive inside the Corbett sectors is accompanied by a trained local nature guide who joins your vehicle at the entry gate. The nature guide fee (approx. ₹1,000 per excursion slot) is not included in vehicle or resort stay packages.

Guests pay the guide charge directly in cash to the assigned guide at the entry gate prior to starting the drive.

4. Natural Habitat & Wildlife Observation Note

Jim Corbett is an open, unfenced natural woodland habitat rather than an enclosed park. Because wild animals—including tigers, elephants, leopards, and birds—move freely across vast forest corridors according to weather and season, specific sightings cannot be promised on any drive. Every excursion is offered as a holistic nature and forest experience.

5. Weather Conditions, Seasonal Track Closures & Force Majeure

Entry gates, woodland tracks, or specific sectors may occasionally adjust timings or pause operations due to heavy rainfall, rising stream levels, track maintenance, or local safety notices. In such natural or unforeseen circumstances beyond private operator control, our travel desk assists guests with alternative local sightseeing or schedule adjustments wherever feasible.

6. Vehicle Seating & Timely Departure

Each 4x4 open Gypsy comfortably accommodates up to 6 adult guests (plus up to 2 children below 5 years), along with the driver and nature guide. To enjoy the full excursion window and reach the entry gate on schedule, guests are requested to be ready at their resort reception 30–45 minutes before departure time.

7. Refund, Modification & Cancellation Policy

Private resort stays and local vehicle transfers coordinated through our travel desk follow clear, straightforward modification and cancellation terms:

  • Private 4x4 Gypsy & Local Transfer Cancellations: Cancellation requests received 7 or more days before the scheduled trip date are eligible for a refund of private vehicle and local coordination charges (less standard payment gateway or administrative handling costs). Cancellations made within 7 days of the travel date or missed pickups are non-refundable.
  • Private Resort & Hotel Bookings: Resort stays follow the respective property's seasonal cancellation schedule. Bookings made for peak holiday periods (such as long weekends, festival holidays, Christmas, and New Year dates) are generally non-refundable once confirmed.
  • Statutory Entry & Forest Lodge Tariffs: Statutory gate entry or forest lodge charges (at actuals, with zero markup by us) are governed strictly by park management rules and are non-refundable and non-transferable once scheduled.
  • Refund Timeline: Approved refunds for eligible private transport or resort cancellations are credited back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.
