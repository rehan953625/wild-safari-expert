Heart of the Dhikala Meadows

What Makes an Overnight Stay at Dhikala So Memorable?

Spending a night at the Dhikala Forest Rest House (FRH) campus is a bucket-list experience for nature lovers, wildlife photographers, and quiet travellers. Located roughly 32 km inside from Dhangarhi Gate, the historic Dhikala complex overlooks the sweeping Ramganga reservoir and wide open Chaurs (savannah grasslands).

Unlike modern city hotels, Dhikala offers a peaceful, minimalist woodland stay enclosed by a protective solar-fenced perimeter. From the campus viewing deck, guests can watch herds of spotted deer and elephants grazing across the valley while listening to evening birdcalls and distant forest sounds.

📅 Visiting Season The Dhikala lodge campus welcomes overnight guests from 15th November to 15th June each year and rests during the monsoon rains.
🚙 4x4 Gypsy Travel Personal cars do not enter the forest trail. Guests travel in a dedicated 4x4 open Gypsy with an experienced local driver from Ramnagar for their stay and drives.

🌲 Other Scenic Forest Lodges Along the Dhikala Route

Alongside the main Dhikala complex, travellers also explore three quieter woodland rest houses located along the Dhangarhi-to-Dhikala trail:

Gairal Lodge (~18 km inside) Set right beside the Ramganga River near Crocodile Pool; prized for riverside birding and peaceful valley views.
Sarapduli Lodge (~24 km inside) Located midway between Gairal and Dhikala amidst rich riverine woodland; a favourite base for keen wildlife trackers.
Sultan Lodge (~8 km inside) The first woodland rest house past Dhangarhi Gate, tucked inside tall Sal groves with simple solar lighting and calm surroundings.
Life Inside the Forest Campus

Dining, Solar Power & Campus Etiquette at Dhikala

Forest lodges focus on eco-friendly simplicity rather than resort luxuries. Knowing how dining, power, and gate timings work helps you pack and plan with ease.

🍽️ Canteen Dining & Meals

  • Wholesome Vegetarian Buffet: The campus canteen serves fresh, home-style vegetarian breakfast, lunch, and dinner.
  • Strictly Veg & Alcohol-Free: To maintain forest serenity, only vegetarian meals are served and alcohol is not allowed inside the reserve.
  • Tea & Light Refreshments: Hot morning tea and evening snacks are available at the central dining courtyard after drives.

⚡ Solar Lighting & Digital Detox

  • Evening Power Window: Rooms use solar lighting, with main power backup running for about 4 hours in the evening (typically 6:00 PM to 10:00 PM).
  • Unplugged Nature Stay: Mobile network coverage is minimal inside the valley, offering a genuine break from city screens.
  • Battery Tip: Carry a fully charged power bank and extra camera batteries, and charge your devices during evening power hours.

🚧 Gate Timings & Campus Safety

  • Daylight Check-In at Dhangarhi: Afternoon entry through Dhangarhi Gate closes by 4:00 PM in winter and 4:30 PM in summer.
  • Stay Inside the Fenced Campus: Guests relax within the solar-fenced compound and explore outside trails only aboard their 4x4 Gypsy.
  • Peaceful Environment: Keep voices soft, avoid loud music, and carry back all non-biodegradable wrappers.

Step-by-Step Preparation

How to Plan Your Dhikala Overnight Trip & Gypsy Transfer

Because the Dhikala campus has a small number of rooms and high seasonal demand, planning your dates and vehicle transfer early ensures a smooth trip.

1. Check Seasonal Dates

Lodge availability and seasonal updates are published for the November-to-June season.

2. Plan 45 to 90 Days Ahead

Advance windows typically open 45 days ahead for Indian guests and 90 days ahead for international travellers. Weekday dates often offer better availability than weekends.

3. Keep Photo IDs Handy

Keep the exact names, ages, and photo ID details ready for every guest in your group, and carry the same original ID card for check-in at Dhangarhi Gate.

4. Coordinate Your 4x4 Gypsy

Once your stay dates are set, connect with our Ramnagar travel desk to arrange your dedicated 4x4 open Gypsy, station/hotel pickup, and daily meadow drives.

Common Visitor Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Dhikala Stays

Practical answers to help you pack and prepare for an overnight stay in the Dhikala valley.

1. Is a Dhikala overnight stay comfortable for families and children?
Yes, it is family-friendly. The entire Dhikala rest house compound is enclosed by a solar-powered perimeter fence and attended by campus staff. Families enjoy watching deer and birds from the open lawn and deck while following simple campus guidelines.
2. How is the nature guide fee handled during a Dhikala stay?
The guide fee is paid directly at the gate/campus. Guests pay the local nature guide fee (approx. ₹700 to ₹1,000 per drive slot) in cash directly to the naturalist accompanying their Gypsy.
3. Can we drive our personal car or taxi up to Dhikala Lodge?
No. Personal sedans, SUVs, and highway taxis are parked safely in Ramnagar. Entry past Dhangarhi Gate and internal drives across the Dhikala trails are conducted exclusively in registered 4x4 open Gypsies.
4. What essentials should we pack for a Dhikala stay?
Pack earth-toned clothing, warm jackets and gloves for misty winter mornings, a power bank and extra camera batteries, personal toiletries, basic medicines, sufficient cash for canteen meals and guide fees, and your original photo ID card.
5. How many consecutive nights do guests typically stay at Dhikala?
Most travellers plan a 1-night or 2-night stay (up to a maximum of 3 nights) so more visitors get a chance to experience the limited heritage rooms inside the valley.
