Gateway to the Deep Grasslands

Why the Dhikala Canter Excursion Stands Out

Dhikala is celebrated as one of the most scenic landscapes in the Corbett region. Framed by the Shivalik foothills, winding channels of the Ramganga River, and wide open Chaurs (savannah meadows), this valley shelters Royal Bengal tigers, herds of Asian elephants, river gharials, and spotted deer.

Since private 4x4 Jeep drives inside Dhikala are reserved exclusively for guests staying overnight at the interior forest lodges, the 16-seater open-top Canter coach (operated by park management from Ramnagar/Dhangarhi Gate) serves as the dedicated day-excursion option for travellers staying at private resorts and hotels around Ramnagar to explore the deep Dhikala meadows.

🚌 Elevated Seating & Views 16-seater open-air safari coach with raised seating that provides clear panoramic sightlines across tall elephant grass and riverbanks.
⏱️ Excursion Duration Approx. 5 to 5.5 hours round trip (~32 km scenic woodland and riverside drive each way from Dhangarhi Gate to Dhikala).

Daily Schedule & Forest Trail

Canter Departure Timings & 32 km Dhikala Route

Canter coaches depart in two daily windows—Morning and Afternoon. Departure and return hours adjust slightly with seasonal daylight across winter and summer months.

🌅 Morning Window (Dawn to Midday)

  • Boarding Window: ~5:30 AM to 6:00 AM (Ramnagar / Dhangarhi Point)
  • Excursion Hours: ~6:00 AM to 11:30 AM (approx. 5 hours)
  • Why Choose Morning: Soft golden sunrise over Dhikala Chaur, active wildlife along sandy trails, and cool breeze beside the Ramganga river.

🌇 Afternoon Window (Midday to Dusk)

  • Boarding Window: ~11:30 AM to 12:00 PM (Ramnagar / Dhangarhi Point)
  • Excursion Hours: ~12:00 PM to 5:30 PM (approx. 5 hours)
  • Why Choose Afternoon: Elephant herds gathering near the Ramganga waters and big cats resting near shaded river pools during warm afternoon hours.

📍 Boarding Point & Check-In Tips

  • Departure Hub: Ramnagar Reception Center or Dhangarhi Gate (~18 km from Ramnagar along Ranikhet Highway).
  • Early Arrival: Reach the boarding point 30 to 40 minutes ahead of departure for smooth seat check-in.
  • Photo ID & Nature Etiquette: Carry the exact original photo ID card shared during trip planning; remember that wildlife sightings occur naturally and are never guaranteed.

🗺️ The 32 km Scenic Trail: Dhangarhi Gate to Dhikala Meadow

Once aboard at Dhangarhi Gate, the open Canter coach winds through five memorable landmarks along the valley floor:

  1. Dhangarhi Entry Point: Starting check-in hub, souvenir corner & natural history interpretation display.
  2. Sultan Woodland Trail (~8 km): Tall Sal canopy and seasonal stream beds known for sambar deer, langurs, and leopards.
  3. Gairal Valley & Crocodile Pool (~18 km): The trail meets the Ramganga River, offering views of basking mugger crocodiles and sleek gharials.
  4. Champion Road & High Bank: Elevated riverside cliffs overlooking deep crystal pools where wild elephants and mahseer fish gather.
  5. Dhikala Chaur & Rest Stop (~31 km): Sweeping savannah grasslands, wooden watchtowers, and a short refreshment break at the fenced Dhikala complex before the return drive.
